# Installing ZoomyPOS on Your Phone

ZoomyPOS works like a regular app on your phone — no App Store needed. Just follow the steps below for your device.

> **Note:** You only need WiFi the first time you open it. After that, it works even without internet.

---

## On Google Chrome

1. Open **Google Chrome** on your phone
2. Go to: **zoomy-pos.vercel.app**
3. Wait for the page to fully load
4. Tap the **Share button** beside the URL
5. Tap **"View More"**
6. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
7. The app will appear on your home screen — open it from there from now on

---

## On Safari

1. Open **Safari** on your iPhone
2. Go to: **zoomy-pos.vercel.app**
3. Wait for the page to fully load
4. Tap the **three dots ( ⋯ )** beside the URL
5. Tap **"Share"**
6. Tap **"View More"**
7. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
8. The app will appear on your home screen — open it from there from now on

---

## Tips

- **First time only:** Open the app and leave it on the main screen for a few seconds while connected to WiFi. This lets it download everything it needs to work offline.
- **No internet after that:** Once set up, the app works without WiFi or mobile data.
- **Your data is saved on your phone** — transactions and products are stored locally and won't disappear if you close the app.
- **To update the app:** If you're told a new version is available, simply open the app while connected to WiFi and it will update automatically.
